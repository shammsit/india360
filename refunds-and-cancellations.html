PAYMENT POLICY

Refunds & Cancellations

This policy explains how India360 handles the ₹1.00 State Partner application submission fee.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Application fee

The application submission fee is ₹1.00 (INR). It covers processing and administration of an application submission. Payment does not guarantee selection.

2. Applicant cancellation

An applicant may stop before paying. Once payment succeeds and the application enters processing, the fee is generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or where a duplicate or erroneous charge is confirmed.

3. Failed or pending payments

If money appears debited but the application remains pending, contact support with the Cashfree order ID. We will verify the transaction before further action. Please do not immediately make another payment.

4. Duplicate or erroneous charges

For multiple successful charges for the same application, email the relevant order IDs. After verification, an eligible duplicate or erroneous payment will be refunded to the original payment method.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method. The time for the credit to appear depends on the bank, card issuer, UPI provider or payment instrument.

6. Request support

Email contact_us@techgen.online with applicant name, registered email, Cashfree order ID, payment date and a description of the issue. Never send card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, passwords or OTPs.
