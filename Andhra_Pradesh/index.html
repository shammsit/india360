About Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is a state in southeastern India, renowned for its rich history, classical arts, traditional cuisine, spiritual destinations and scenic coastline along the Bay of Bengal. Its cultural identity reflects centuries of heritage, architecture, literature and traditions.
